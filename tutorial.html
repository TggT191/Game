Tutorial Complete!

You are ready for real battles. Here is everything covered:

  • Main menu — pick Play, Tutorial, or Build Deck (save a 7-card deck).
  • Rock Paper Scissors — each round starts with RPS to decide who goes first.
  • Hand & energy — each card costs ⚡ Energy. HP and energy bars are at the top.
  • Type matchups — strong = 1.5× damage, weak = 0.5×, Neutral = 1×. Dark is special: it deals 1.75× to everything and takes 1.75× from everything. Your element badge (top right) updates to the type of the last card you played.
  • Deck pile (∞) — tap the center pile to draw 1 card from your saved Build Deck. This does not end your turn. A full hand (7 cards) swaps one random slot for the new card.
  • Shuffle — spends your turn to add 1 random card from the full card pool. Same 7-card swap rule if your hand is already full. Light ✨ and Dark 🌑 cards are rare in the shuffle pool.
  • Auto-refill — an empty hand refills automatically from your saved Build Deck.
  • Items — use any item without ending your turn. Items carry over between rounds in one run and reset when you return to the main menu (Clear Game Run).
  • Round rewards — after winning a round, choose one of 3 items. Boss rounds (every 5th) give stronger loot and tougher opponents.
  • Win / Lose — reduce the opponent HP to 0 to win a round. Player HP is 400; normal opponents are 400 HP and bosses (every 5th round) are 700 HP. If your HP hits 0 and you have a Cinder Plume (Phoenix Feather) saved, it revives you automatically — use it wisely, it only triggers once per game run.
  • Reload protection — reloading or using the browser back button during a battle sends you straight back to the main menu so you never rejoin a stale session.
